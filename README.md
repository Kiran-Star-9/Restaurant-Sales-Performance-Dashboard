🍽️ Restaurant Sales Performance Dashboard

An interactive Restaurant Sales Performance Dashboard built using Microsoft Power BI to analyze restaurant revenue, order volume, customer ratings, payment methods, and menu performance.

The dashboard transforms raw restaurant sales data into an interactive business-reporting solution that helps stakeholders understand sales trends, identify high-performing dishes and categories, and monitor overall restaurant performance.

---

📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing restaurant sales performance across different months, dishes, categories, and payment methods.

The dashboard provides a clear overview of key business metrics while allowing users to interact with the data through slicers and dynamic visualizations.

---

🎯 Business Questions

The dashboard is designed to answer questions such as:

- What is the total revenue generated?
- How many orders were placed?
- Which month generated the highest and lowest revenue?
- Which dishes generate the most revenue?
- Which food category contributes the most revenue?
- What payment methods are most commonly used?
- How does revenue change over time?

---

🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Power BI Desktop — Dashboard development and data visualization
- Power Query — Data cleaning and transformation
- DAX — Calculated measures and analytical metrics
- Data Visualization — KPI cards, line charts, bar charts, donut charts, and slicers

---

📊 Dashboard Features

KPI Cards

The dashboard includes key performance indicators such as:

- Total Revenue: $1.497M
- Total Orders: 2K
- Average Rating: 4.05

Revenue by Month

A line chart tracks monthly revenue across the year and helps identify sales peaks and lower-performing periods.

Key observation: Revenue peaked in May, while August recorded the lowest revenue.

Revenue by Dish

A bar chart compares revenue generated by individual dishes.

Key observation: Seekh Kebab and Palak Paneer are among the highest revenue-generating dishes.

Revenue by Payment Method

A donut chart shows the distribution of revenue across:

- Cash
- Card
- UPI

The visualization helps identify the contribution of different payment methods to overall revenue.

Revenue by Category

A bar chart compares revenue across food categories.

Key observation: The Main Course category is the largest revenue contributor, generating more than $563K.

---

🎛️ Interactive Filters

The dashboard includes interactive slicers that allow users to explore the data dynamically.

- Month Slicer — Filter dashboard results by month
- Payment Method Slicer — Filter results by Cash, Card, or UPI

These filters update the dashboard visuals based on the selected values.

---

💡 Key Business Insights

- Revenue Trend: Monthly revenue fluctuates throughout the year, with May showing a notable sales peak.
- Category Performance: Main Course is the largest revenue-generating category.
- Dish Performance: Seekh Kebab and Palak Paneer are among the top revenue-generating dishes.
- Payment Behavior: Revenue is distributed across Cash, Card, and UPI, indicating the use of multiple payment options.
- Menu Performance: Lower-revenue dishes such as Dhal Fry and Biryani can be monitored for further menu-performance analysis.

🚀 How to Use

1. Download the ".pbix" file from this repository.
2. Open the file using Microsoft Power BI Desktop.
3. If prompted, refresh the data source.
4. Use the Month and Payment Method slicers to interact with the dashboard.
5. Explore the visualizations to analyze restaurant sales performance.
